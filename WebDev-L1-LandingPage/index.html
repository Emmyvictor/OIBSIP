How it works

  1. Pick a route and time

    Choose a departure from the live timetable. Seats left are shown on every sailing.

  2. Pay and get your QR code

    Pay by card or bank transfer. Your ticket arrives by SMS and email.

  3. Show the code and board

    Scan at the jetty, put on your life jacket and take any open seat.
